About

Architecting Tomorrow's Enterprise

Sagar Gupta is a leading AI engineer, security practitioner, and cloud architect with over 14 years of experience designing and delivering transformative technology for enterprise environments worldwide.

Renowned for his ability to architect secure, scalable systems, he specialises in integrating artificial intelligence with business-critical software, championing best practices in cybersecurity and cloud strategy. His career spans global enterprises and high-growth startups, where he has consistently delivered at the intersection of innovation and operational excellence.

Sagar is a frequent speaker at industry conferences and an advocate for ethical AI, sustainable software development, and open-source security practices. He mentors engineers and contributes to the broader tech community.
